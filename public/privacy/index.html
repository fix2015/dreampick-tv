Privacy Policy

DreamPick TV · Last updated: 6 October 2026

DreamPick TV is a "pick your dream" quiz game for the whole family. We designed it to work without collecting personal information. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

1. Information we collect

None. DreamPick TV has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stars earned, games played, chosen answers and the sound on/off setting) is saved only in your device's local storage so it is there the next time you open the app. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

3. Internet access

Question images and sound effects are downloaded from our content server (Amazon Web Services, EU region) when you play. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to the server for the duration of the download; we do not log or store it. The app needs an internet connection to load content.

4. Links to other services

The app contains a link to the DreamPick TV channel on YouTube. Tapping it opens YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app, which are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We receive no information about what you do there.

5. Children

DreamPick TV is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country).

6. Permissions

The app requests no device permissions beyond internet access.

7. Changes

If we ever change what the app does with data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
